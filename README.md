Relay Collective is a technology-driven social impact initiative focused on breaking down barriers that prevent vulnerable individuals from accessing essential services.

What began as a hackathon project at <b>FaithTech's HACKVAN 2026</b>, developed in collaboration with <b>The Salvation Army – Belkin Communities of Hope</b>, 
has evolved into a long-term initiative with a mission to make transportation more accessible, reliable, and inclusive.

Through Relay Collective, we're continuing to develop technology that connects community organizations, volunteer drivers, and the people who need them most.
