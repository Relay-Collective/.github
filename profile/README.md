<img src="./assets/title-platform.svg" width="100%" alt="03, The platform. Built once. Shared by everyone.">

Nonprofits do extraordinary work, but too often they do it alone, each with its own tools, its own volunteer lists, and its own resources. **Relay Collective is the infrastructure they share:** organizations, teams, roles, and requests on one open foundation, with services on top. Every new service, from transportation to volunteering to resource sharing, starts from what the others have already proven.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Relay-Collective/Relay-Collective/main/docs/images/platform.png" width="100%" alt="One foundation, many services. Transportation: working today. Expanding services, including volunteer roles, resource sharing, and cross-organization services: planned. All built on a shared foundation of organizations, roles, and requests.">

<img src="./assets/title-ride.svg" width="100%" alt="04, The first service: transportation. One ride, start to finish.">

Transportation is the first service built on the foundation, and it shows how the whole collective works: an organization makes a request, a verified volunteer answers it, and everyone can see it through to the end.

<img src="./assets/journey.svg" width="100%" alt="1. Staff book a ride: front-line staff book a ride for someone they support, in under a minute. 2. A driver accepts: the offer goes to reviewed volunteer drivers, one accepts, and staff see it. 3. Picked up at the door: the driver collects the client, and the ride stays in view the whole way. 4. Arrived, help reached: drop-off is confirmed, and the appointment that matters is kept. If no driver is available, staff see it right away with a next step: ask drivers again, change the time, or send on transit.">

<img src="./assets/title-story.svg" width="100%" alt="07, Our story. From a hackathon to a platform.">

Relay Collective began at **FaithTech HACKVAN 2026**, built alongside **The Salvation Army – Belkin Communities of Hope** in Vancouver, BC. Front-line staff showed us how often people miss out on help that already exists, so we started with the most common barrier, **getting there**. The vision was always bigger: **one foundation where nonprofits share what they have and volunteers find where they're needed.** Within weeks, a hackathon project became that foundation.

<img src="./assets/timeline.svg" width="100%" alt="Our story so far. 2026: born at HACKVAN, a FaithTech hackathon project built with The Salvation Army – Belkin Communities of Hope. October 4: version 1.0 ships as CareRide, with live ride status, route previews, and printable transit slips. October 21: CareRide becomes Relay Collective. October 25: workspaces for organizations, volunteers, and admins. October 29: teams, roles, and join requests. October 31, today: version 1.6 and a new identity. Next, planned: resource sharing, more volunteer roles, and cross-organization services.">

<p align="center"><sub>Relay Collective is an independent platform, open to any organization. The Salvation Army is our pilot partner, not our owner.</sub></p>
